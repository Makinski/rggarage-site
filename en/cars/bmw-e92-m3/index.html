Why Single Hump and Manual Matter

Many E92 M3 enthusiasts specifically look for the cleaner, more analogue interior layout. The dashboard without navigation gives the cabin a more focused feel, while the single hump layout is considered a desirable detail by a part of the market. Combined with the 6-speed manual gearbox, it creates a more authentic and enthusiast-led M3 configuration.
